My Passion for Football

My passion for football

Football is one of my favourite sports since I was 5 years old. As soon as I learned to play it, I was keen on being outside to play with my friends.

Why I enjoy football?

Having been a hyperactive child from an early age, football has taught me the importance of teamwork, resilience, and always giving my best on the pitch.

My weekly routine

I try to maintain a healthy lifestyle and have a diet rich in carbs and protein so that I can stay injury-free and continue playing as much as possible. I cycle every day for one hour and I attend one-hour gym classes focusing on strength training, HIIT and lifting from Monday to Thursday.
